# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. Whether you are a first-time contributor or a long-standing one, we value your participation. This code of conduct sets out what we expect from everyone who takes part in the project, and what happens when those expectations are not met.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful**: Use welcoming and inclusive language. Treat others the way you would want to be treated.
- **Being constructive**: Give thoughtful feedback and acknowledge different perspectives.
- **Being patient**: English is not everyone's first language, and written communication is easily misread.
- **Being collaborative**: Work together to solve problems and improve the project.
- **Being transparent**: Communicate openly and honestly about your ideas, concerns and limitations.
- **Acknowledging mistakes**: When you make a mistake, own it and help others learn from it.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination based on protected characteristics (race, gender, religion, nationality, sexual orientation, disability, and so on)
- Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuro(a)typicality, physical appearance, body size, race or religion
- Deliberate misgendering, or use of "dead" or rejected names
- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Violent behavior or language directed against another person
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above
- Sustained disruption of project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Stalking, doxing or threats of violence
- Trolling or intentionally derailing discussions
- Publishing others' private information without their consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior can be reported by:

1. **Contacting the maintainer** through GitHub: [@3dg1luk43](https://github.com/3dg1luk43). Mention the maintainer in the thread concerned, or use the contact details on the profile for anything you would rather not raise in public.
2. **Reporting the content** with GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request.
3. **Flagging it publicly** in the relevant issue, pull request or discussion, if you are comfortable doing so.

All reports are reviewed and handled as discreetly as the situation allows.

### Our Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and is expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instance of unacceptable behavior.

The maintainer has the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this code of conduct
- Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful
- Communicate the reason for moderation actions when appropriate, and give the person an opportunity to address concerns

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- The GitHub repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and comments
- Any other place where project business is discussed, such as the Home Assistant community forum
- Private messages related to project business

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or community, such as speaking on behalf of the project.

## Consequences

Unacceptable behavior will not be tolerated. Depending on its severity and frequency, consequences may include:

1. **Warning**: A private or public message explaining the violation
2. **Temporary restriction**: Temporary loss of comment or contribution privileges
3. **Permanent ban**: Permanent removal from the project's spaces
